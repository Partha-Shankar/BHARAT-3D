# BHARAT 3D — Logo generation prompts (ChatGPT / image AI)

## How to use

1. Paste **one** numbered prompt below into ChatGPT (or your image model) as a single message—do not split it.
2. Request a **square** composition (1:1), **vector-like flat** mark: clean edges, no gradients unless very subtle.
3. Background: **warm paper `#f3efe6`** or **transparent**; no desk mockups, no framed posters, no browser chrome.
4. Output should read as a **logo mark**, not an illustration: minimal detail, high contrast, works at **32×32 px** favicon size and on **letterhead**.
5. Palette only: **navy `#1e4d6b`**, **teal `#1f7a72`**, **warm paper `#f3efe6`** (plus white or near-black for contrast if needed—sparingly).
6. Iterate with “simpler, fewer lines, thicker strokes” if the first result is too busy.
7. Export or ask for SVG-style simplicity: flat fills, no shadows, no 3D glossy rendering.

## What to reject

- Any use of the **Indian flag** or **tricolor** as branding.
- **Map of India silhouette** dominating the mark (small abstract survey geometry is fine; country outline as the logo is not).
- **Globe**, **circuit-brain “smart city”**, **glass orb**, or **generic stock skyline**.
- **Emoji**, mascots, or playful cartoon treatment unfit for government-grade registry.
- **3D glossy renders**, metallic bevels, lens flare, or **photographic** / **photorealistic city** scenes.
- **Mockups** (laptop, signage in situ, 3D building renders) instead of a flat logo on paper or transparent background.
